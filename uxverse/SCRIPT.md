# Into the UX-Verse

A portfolio film for **Rohit Patle**, Senior UX/UI Designer and AI UX Designer.
Runtime 3:20 · 1920×1080 · 30fps · score at 120 BPM felt in half time (4-second chords)

**Sources.** Resume (Sept 2026) is the source of truth for roles, dates, numbers and certifications. Orchard Studio and Wall of Fame come from Rohit's own description. The UX laws are the ones he teaches on Instagram at @rohitpatle03.

## The idea

A space adventure told like a comic book that learned to move. Rohit is the hero, an SVG character with a jetpack, a black tee with a white crescent, and a beard. His career is a flight path through the **UX-Verse**, where UX laws behave like the laws of physics and the scariest thing in space is the design-to-dev **handoff black hole**.

## Three visual worlds

| World | When | Look | Frame rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Paper** | The past: Nagpur, college, first job | Cut paper, torn edges, drop shadows, hand-made wobble | Stop motion at 8fps, every piece boils |
| **3D space** | Career in orbit | three.js with toon shading, ink outlines, halftone dots, CMYK misregistration | Camera on ones (30fps) |
| **Comic layer** | Everywhere | Caption boxes, speech bubbles, onomatopoeia, panels, page curls | Rohit animates on twos (12fps), the Spider-Verse way |

The jump from paper to 3D happens at liftoff: the paper world tears open and the rocket punches through into rendered space.

**Palette** is offset-print CMYK plus paper: cyan `#00C2FF`, magenta `#FF2E88`, yellow `#FFD23F`, ink `#0B0A12`, paper `#EFE3C8`.
**Type:** Bangers for sound effects, Kalam (by the Indian Type Foundry) for narration, Anton for titles, Space Mono for the ship HUD.

## Score

Synthesized in the browser, written in the language of a Hans Zimmer space score without copying any of his themes: a pipe organ, a ticking clock, 16th-note string ostinatos, BRAAAM brass, taiko, a choir, and Shepard-tone risers that feel like they climb forever. A minor, with the chords Am, F, C and G, four seconds each. The paper chapters switch to piano, pizzicato and a music box. The finale ends on the classic bVI, bVII, I lift into **A major**.

## 05 · The Laws of the UX-Verse (1:18 to 1:44)
**Splash:** "THE LAWS OF THE UX-VERSE"
**Caption:** "Out here, UX laws work like physics. I teach them on Instagram. @rohitpatle03"
Six panels, one law each, each with a hit on the downbeat:
1. **Fitts's Law.** "Big, close targets are faster to hit." A comet cursor nails a big button (**BOOP!**), then misses a tiny far one (**WHIFF**).
2. **Hick's Law.** "More choices, slower decisions." Twenty-four buttons make Rohit sweat. Three buttons make him grin.
3. **Miller's Law.** "Chunk it. Working memory holds about seven things." A line of ten digits snaps into three chunks.
4. **Jakob's Law.** "People expect your product to work like the ones they already use." A row of round planets and one confused cube.
5. **Doherty Threshold.** "Answer in under 400ms and people stay in flow." Green at 380ms. Asleep at 2,400ms.
6. **Von Restorff Effect.** "The one that's different is the one they remember." One magenta star in a field of white.
**Caption:** "Break them and users feel it. Follow them and nobody notices. That's the job."

## 08 · The Handoff Black Hole (2:40 to 3:00)
**Caption:** "Every ship out here fears one thing."
**3D:** A black hole with a burning accretion disk. Design cards spiral in and stretch into noodles.
**Caption:** "The handoff. Where good designs go to die."
Two ships shout across the void. DESIGN: "It's 16px!" DEV: "The spec said 13?"
**2:50** Rohit flies into the middle. **"I speak both."** Silence, then the drop.
He lays a bridge of glowing tiles across the hole. Each tile pairs a design token with its code: `space/16 ↔ --space-4`, `color/brand ↔ --brand`, `radius/12 ↔ --radius-md`, `Button/Primary ↔ <Button/>`. The cards ride across intact.
**Caption:** "Tokens, components, annotated specs. Nothing gets lost."
