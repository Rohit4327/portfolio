# Hello, World: Rohit's portfolio film

Runtime 3:04 · 1920×1080 · 120 BPM (one beat = 0.5s, one bar = 2s)

**The idea.** Every engineer's first program prints `hello, world`. The film opens on that line in a terminal, then restyles it like a designer would. That one move is the whole story: someone who started with logic, learned to care how things feel, and now works in the gap between design and code where most products lose their quality.

**The type system tells the story on its own.**

| Voice | Face | Color | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Engineer | JetBrains Mono | Terminal amber `#FFB547` | Logic, code, the degree |
| Designer | Instrument Serif italic | Bone `#EEEAE3` | Feel, taste, craft |
| Rohit / the bridge | Bricolage Grotesque | Amber to Figma blue gradient | Both at once |

Figma's selection blue `#0D99FF` and its redline orange `#F24822` show up whenever the film is "in the design tool".
